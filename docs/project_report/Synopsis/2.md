# 2. Objectives of the Project

This section outlines the proposed objectives for Focus Hub, stated in planned terms and presented at a high level.

Primary Objective
To design and propose an AI-enabled alumni collaboration platform that facilitates structured networking, collaborative learning, and knowledge discovery for alumni, students, and educators.

Specific Aims (high-level)
- Establish a centralized environment for academic networking and profile management.
- Provide mechanisms for synchronous and asynchronous communication to support engagement.
- Introduce AI-assisted question answering to improve access to institutional knowledge.
- Enable curated sharing of educational resources and materials to support collaboration.
- Incorporate governance features for moderation and role-based access in the community.

The emphasis is on defining measurable aims and anticipated capabilities rather than implementation specifics.

---

# 3. Scope of the Project

Scope Overview
The proposal covers the conceptual design and functional requirements for a full-stack, web-accessible platform that integrates social collaboration with AI-assisted knowledge tools for educational communities.

In-Scope (conceptual)
- User lifecycle: registration, profile representation, and role definitions.
- Community interactions: feeds, discussions, and moderated forums.
- Knowledge services: AI-enabled Q&A and searchable knowledge artifacts.
- Resource exchange: mechanisms for sharing and indexing learning materials.
- Administrative controls: policy-driven moderation and analytics at a high level.

Out-of-Scope (for initial proposal)
- Low-level implementation details, specific technology stack decisions, and deployment scripts. These will be addressed in subsequent technical sections.

Anticipated Extensions
Future phases may evaluate integrations such as real-time multimedia conferencing, career services, and advanced personalization; these are noted as optional extensions for later development.

# 4. Existing System Analysis

Summary
Contemporary alumni management solutions predominantly provide record-keeping, announcement distribution, and limited messaging. Commercial social platforms offer general-purpose communication but lack structures tailored to academic collaboration and institutional knowledge management.

Analytical Observations
- Fragmentation: Institutions often rely on multiple disconnected services for networking, discussion, and resource sharing.
- Insufficient intelligence: Few existing platforms provide domain-aware or institution-specific knowledge retrieval.
- Limited governance: Administrative and moderation features are often inadequate for academic community needs.

These observations motivate a purpose-built platform that aligns social interaction modalities with academic workflows.

---

# 4.1 Limitations of Existing Systems

Key Limitations (concise)
- Limited synchronous collaboration and real-time coordination features.
- Absence of integrated, institution-aware AI assistance for knowledge discovery.
- Fragmented user experience across multiple external services.
- Insufficient moderation and policy enforcement mechanisms for academic contexts.
- Scalability and data governance concerns in legacy or ad-hoc systems.

Conclusion
The proposed Focus Hub is positioned to mitigate these limitations by delivering a unified, policy-compliant environment for alumni and academic collaboration. Detailed design and technical validation are reserved for later sections of the report.
